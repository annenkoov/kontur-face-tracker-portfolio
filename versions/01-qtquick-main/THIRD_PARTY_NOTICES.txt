FER+ emotion recognition model
The application downloads the pre-trained emotion-ferplus-7.onnx model from
the ONNX Model Zoo for local inference. Model documentation and license:
https://github.com/onnx/models/tree/main/validated/vision/body_analysis/emotion_ferplus
The model expects 64x64 grayscale face crops and produces eight expression
scores. It is not a stress classifier or a medical diagnostic model.
